# Landing Page

Build the page around one audience and one primary conversion.

## Recommended flow
1. Hero: outcome, differentiation, primary CTA.
2. Immediate proof: recognizable trust signal, metric, customer, demo, or product evidence.
3. Problem/context.
4. Product mechanism or feature story.
5. Benefits expressed as outcomes.
6. Evidence: case studies, metrics, testimonials, screenshots.
7. Objection handling.
8. Final CTA.

## Rules
- Make the headline concrete.
- Do not overload the hero.
- Show the product when possible.
- Repeat the primary CTA at natural decision points.
- Use visual storytelling instead of endless feature cards.
- Keep copy scannable.
- Give each section a distinct purpose.
- Avoid fake metrics or testimonials.

## Finish
Review message clarity, CTA hierarchy, credibility, mobile flow, page speed, SEO basics, and visual memorability.
